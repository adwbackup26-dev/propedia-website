About

Real estate done with
precision and purpose.

I'm Anirudha Warhadpande, a licensed REALTOR® with HomeLife Miracle Realty Ltd., Brokerage, operating across Mississauga and the broader Greater Toronto Area. My practice focuses on residential rentals and sales — connecting serious buyers, sellers, and tenants with the right properties through a process that is transparent, efficient, and results-driven.

My background spans both corporate operations and real estate, giving me an analytical edge that most agents don't bring to the table. I approach every transaction with the same discipline — understanding the numbers, reading the market, and protecting your interests at every step.

Whether you're a first-time renter navigating a competitive GTA market, a buyer looking for your next home, or a landlord seeking a qualified tenant, I provide the same level of care and professionalism at every price point.
